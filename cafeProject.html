Our Story

Welcome to Krushna's World

Located in the heart of the city, Krushna Café started with a simple dream: to create a space where coffee isn't just a drink, but a conversation starter.


Whether you are a student looking for a quiet study corner, or friends catching up after a long week, our warm lighting and aroma of freshly ground beans welcome you home.
